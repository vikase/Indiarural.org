Rural Telangana, India · since 1996

Good schools for village children in rural Telangana.

In 1996, eighty children in one Telangana village started school with ten teachers. Today the Rural Development Foundation runs four schools and a junior college for 2,824 students, half of them girls. IRDF is the American charity that helps pay for it, with no paid staff and nothing taken off the top.

The story

Thirty years, one village at a time.

The Rural Development Foundation (RDF) was founded in Hyderabad in 1996 by the Erraballi family to bring a good school to their own village. The same year, family and friends in California chartered the India Rural Development Fund so that Americans could give to it with a US tax deduction.

The model has not changed. RDF runs the schools: hires the teachers, cooks the mid-day meal, keeps half the seats for girls, and admits by lottery so every caste and community in the village is represented. IRDF raises money in the United States and sends it as grants. All of IRDF's officers volunteer, so the only costs are bank fees and state filings.

The schools are not charity schools in the usual sense. Families pay modest fees where they can, about one in ten students gets a concession, and the schools compete and win against the best in the district in archery, athletics, kabaddi and science fairs. Alumni have represented India at the junior world championships and the Olympics.

I have been working in developing countries for 25 years and have never before encountered an organization so deserving of support.Glenn Davis Stone, anthropologist, Washington University in St. Louis, who founded the Village India program at the Kalleda junior college

The schools

Five institutions in three districts.

All run by RDF from Hyderabad with local village committees. Counts are for the 2025–26 year.

What school does

Three of the stories behind the numbers.

2,824 children are in school this year. Here are three things that happened because they were.

2008

From a village school to the Beijing Olympics

Pranitha Vardhineni learned archery at the Kalleda school. In 2006 she won silver for India at the junior world championships in Mexico; in 2008 she shot for India at the Beijing Olympics. Her schoolmate Adonda Raju took bronze in Mexico.

1998

A school under the trees

Matendla had no school at all. RDF started classes outdoors, under the palm trees, before there was a building. Today it has 840 students, science and computer labs, a dining hall, a school bus and its own clean-water plant.

US

A summer at an American high school

RDF students have been chosen for the YES exchange program, spending a summer at a US high school. Two fourth-graders from Rollakal were selected for the state sports school at Hakimpet in 2019.

The money

A dollar goes a long way in rural Telangana.

IRDF has no office, no salaries and no fundraising costs. What you give is what the schools get, less a card fee. Here is roughly what it buys.

$25

A month of mid-day meals for one child: rice, dal and vegetables from the school garden.

$100

Books and two uniforms for a child for the year. The uniform is what erases caste and class in the classroom.

$250

One child's whole year: fees, meals, books and uniform.

$3,000

A teacher's salary for a year.

$10,000

A science lab, a library or a school bus.

$25,000

A new classroom block.
